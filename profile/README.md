# EthioNutri AI

## Project Description

**EthioNutri AI** is an AI-powered nutrition and meal planning platform built around Ethiopian foods, eating habits, and fasting practices. It delivers personalized nutrition, meal, and exercise recommendations based on each user's health profile, dietary restrictions, health conditions, goals, and lifestyle.

## Problem

Most nutrition apps rely on foreign food databases and focus mainly on calorie counting. They often lack Ethiopian foods such as **injera, shiro, and tibs**, and provide limited support for fasting practices and individual health conditions.

**EthioNutri AI** solves this by combining Ethiopian and international food data with AI-powered, personalized recommendations.

## Main Features

- Personalized health profile
- Ethiopian and international food database
- Food logging through camera, voice, or manual entry
- Nutrition tracking
- AI nutrition chatbot
- Personalized meal planning
- Ethiopian Orthodox and Ramadan fasting support
- AI-powered exercise planning
- Grocery planning
- Smart cooking recommendations
- Nutrient deficiency risk monitoring
- Nutritionist supervision and support
- Amharic and English interface
- Light and dark themes
